      towards the end of the book, everything felt so rushed and forced. They

      in a few pages. And the case wasn’t “solved” – no one was actually
      arrested. They just figured out how the crime happened, but not the actual
      individuals involved. But lesson solved, don’t over-leverage yourself with
      tons of business debt. Because you’ll soon get desperate and have a mafia
      crime boss buy your business and then his thugs will eventually kill you
      when your business declares bankruptcy.
