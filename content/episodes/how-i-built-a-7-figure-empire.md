# **Key takeaways**

* Speed beats skill in sales. Responding in 60 seconds vs. 48 hours decides who closes.
* Treat your business like a business, not a side hustle. Off-hours coverage is non-negotiable.• Use BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timing) to qualify and disqualify leads fast.
* Your job as a salesperson is to help the prospect decide — yes or no. Both are wins.
* Revenue = Goodwill × Number of Offers. Most businesses leave money on the table by making just one.
* Talk to your customers. Their problems and complaints are the blueprint for your next offer.
* Focus on LTV, not just CAC. The same customer buying repeatedly will outperform endless cold acquisition.
