# Key takeaways

• Specialization is a losing strategy — being multidisciplinary compounds your network, decision-making, and opportunities over time.

• "Return on time," not hours worked, is the metric that actually matters — build overlapping systems instead of trading time 1:1.

• The thing that feels safest (a stable job inside a big institution) is often now the riskiest position, because those structures are rationalizing down.

• Distribution and scale no longer require outspending competitors — new, cheaper paths to reach people exist now.

• You don't need national scale to build a great business — local, high-margin, high-trust businesses are very viable again.
