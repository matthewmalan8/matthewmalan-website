# Key takeaways

• Beliefs are the root: they drive your thoughts, emotions, actions, and outcomes, not the other way around.

• Events have no inherent meaning. You add the story on top of the raw sense data.

• Affirmations often backfire because a deeper part of you still believes the opposite (oppositional energy).

• Lasting change is subtraction, not addition. Remove the weeds instead of planting flowers over them.

• A belief cleared at the root never returns, the same way you stopped believing in Santa Claus.
