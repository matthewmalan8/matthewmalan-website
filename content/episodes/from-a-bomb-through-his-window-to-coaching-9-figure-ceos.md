# Key takeaways

• Reframe selling as an act of service — people don't want to be sold, but they love being served.

• Trust isn't built over months. People decide in an instant whether you're worth trusting.

• Growth often comes from one focused change — like getting in front of bigger buyers — not ten new tactics at once.

• "Love tough" coaching means caring about someone enough to push them past their fear of selling.

• Qualify hard: determine if the person wants to solve the problem and if you're the right fit before you go all in.

• Advertising doesn't close high-ticket sales on its own — people buy from those they know, like, and trust.
