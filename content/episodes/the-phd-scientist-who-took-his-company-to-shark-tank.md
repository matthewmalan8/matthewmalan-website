# Key takeaways

• Third-party press (magazines, TV, podcasts) carries more weight than paid advertising because it's an endorsement, not a sales pitch.

• Licensing an invention can be more sustainable than building a large company, especially if you value flexibility over scale.

• Rapid growth and outside investment aren't always the goal. Rob turned down Shark Tank money to keep full control of his invention.

• Preparation beats charisma. Rob trained for his Shark Tank pitch the same way he trains for big wave surfing.

• Niching down into a small, defensible market can outlast chasing a "home run" business.
