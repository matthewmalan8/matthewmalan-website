# Key Takeaways

* Radical accountability isn't just a buzzword — it's the moment you stop blaming circumstances and start reverse-engineering your way out.
* The highest form of self-love is having integrity for your future self while living in the present.
* Prison stripped Art of 4,500 contacts and showed him he had no one — and that became the foundation he built everything on.
* Your worst experiences are your greatest qualifications. Art's trauma is exactly why veterans and convicts will trust him as a counselor.
* Make adjustments, not excuses. Purpose doesn't require a perfect path — just a refusal to quit.
